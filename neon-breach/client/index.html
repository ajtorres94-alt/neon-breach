Controls

Mouse & keyboard — WASD move · mouse aim · click fire · right-click aim down sights · R reload · F proximity mine · T laser mine · X throw knife · V melee · Q/E lean · C crouch · Space jump · 1–6 or wheel switch weapon · hold G for the callout wheel (team modes) · Tab scoreboard. Controller — Left stick move · right stick aim · LT aim · RT fire · A jump · B crouch · X reload · Y switch weapon · LB proximity mine · RB throwing knife · left-stick click laser mine · right-stick click melee · D-pad left-right lean · D-pad up callout wheel · View scoreboard · Menu pause.

Briefing — Grab rifles, armor and medkits around the arena. Proximity mines stick to any surface and detonate when an enemy walks past; laser mines project a beam that blows when it is crossed (3 at a time). Knives can be thrown or used up close — strike from behind to kill instantly. The rocket launcher has splash damage, so mind your own feet. In Capture the Flag, carry the enemy flag home while yours is safe. In Intel Recovery every fallen operator drops intel: collect theirs to score, grab your own to deny it. Fresh spawns are protected for a moment — a shimmer marks them.
